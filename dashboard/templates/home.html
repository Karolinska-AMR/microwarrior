About us

Welcome to our project dedicated to combating drug-resistant tuberculosis (TB) through innovative research. At KI, we are deeply committed to addressing one of the most pressing global health challenges of our time. Our multidisciplinary team of experts has harnessed the power of data and cutting-edge technology to develop a solution that aims to predict drug-resistant TB.

Our mission is centered on mitigating the impact of drug-resistant TB on individuals, communities, and healthcare systems. We are striving for a world where TB is no longer a threat, and in achieving this vision, our project plays a pivotal role. It offers key features, such as the early identification of drug-resistant TB to expedite treatment, informing treatment strategies for more effective resistance management, and ongoing research and development efforts aimed at enhancing our predictive algorithms.
